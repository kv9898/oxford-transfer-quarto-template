# Attribution and modifications

This repository adapts the **Oxford Transfer of Status Template** by
**Joseph Rowell and Frank Fu**:

<https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/oxford-transfer-of-status-template/nkbxmjcdhggb>

The original template is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The template was adapted to Quarto by Dianyi Yang in 2026. The adaptation
replaces the standalone multi-file LaTeX workflow with a Quarto PDF format,
removes unused specialist LaTeX dependencies, adds metadata-driven title and
page-style options, and integrates Quarto citations, cross-references, figures,
tables, listings, abstracts, and appendices.
